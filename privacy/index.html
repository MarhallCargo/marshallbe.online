Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Marshall Relay Limited collects through its apps and website, how that information is used, who it is shared with and what choices you have. It is written to be read, not just published.

Last updated: 4 October 2026 Effective: 4 October 2026 Applies to: Marshall VPN, Quiet Escape, Capy Resort and marshallbe.online

At a glance

  • No accounts. None of our apps ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment card.
  • Your data stays on your device. Game progress, settings and purchase status are stored locally. Marshall VPN keeps no logs of your online activity.
  • Ads keep the free versions free. Ads are served by Google AdMob and are non-personalised. In our games, every ad request is configured for child-directed treatment.
  • Payments are handled by Apple and Google. We never see your card details.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for profiling or advertising of our own.

3. Marshall VPN

No account, no personal details

Marshall VPN does not require an account and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, Apple ID or Google account. When you first use the app, it generates a random device identifier and stores it securely on your device (in the iOS Keychain or in encrypted storage on Android). This identifier is not derived from your hardware and is not an advertising identifier.

What our servers receive

To operate the service, the app sends the following to our servers:

  • the device identifier, to issue your VPN access key and to keep track of your subscription or trial status;
  • purchase confirmations from Apple or Google (the product bought and the store’s transaction identifier), so that a subscription or one-time purchase can be activated on your devices;
  • device names that you enter yourself in the device manager, so that you can recognise the devices linked to your subscription;
  • a confirmation that a rewarded video was watched, in the free, ad-supported mode;
  • platform and app version when the app downloads the current server list, so that we can serve a compatible configuration.

Our servers keep these records together: your device identifier, the devices linked to a subscription and their names, subscription status and expiry dates, and the store product and transaction identifiers used to activate purchases. Requests for in-app news contain no identifiers. Like any internet service, our servers also see your IP address while they process a request; we do not keep IP address logs tied to your device identifier.

No activity logs

We do not log your browsing activity, DNS queries, the content of your traffic, connection timestamps or bandwidth usage on our VPN servers. Connection state is processed transiently to keep the tunnel running and is not stored. The tunnel is created on your device by the open-source sing-box engine, using Apple’s NetworkExtension framework on iOS and the system VPN service on Android.

Advertising in the free mode

The free, ad-supported mode shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK): a short rewarded video to connect, and occasional full-screen, banner and native ads. Where the law requires it, the app first shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform). Ads are non-personalised: the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. To deliver, measure and secure ads, Google may process your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and information about the ads shown and your interactions with them. A subscription removes all ads.

Optional features

  • Feedback. The in-app feedback form sends only the text you type, with no identifiers attached.
  • Diagnostics. Log collection is off by default. If you turn it on, logs are stored only on your device until you choose to copy or share them.
  • Server tests. Latency checks and optional service-availability checks are performed directly from your device; no results are sent to us.
  • Third-party subscriptions. If you import a subscription from another provider, the app downloads that provider’s configuration directly from the link you supply, and the servers you connect to are operated by that provider, not by us.
  • Access obtained outside the app. If you obtain or renew access through an automated subscription flow we operate on a third-party platform, we process the pseudonymous identifiers and subscription details that platform provides, solely to issue and manage your access.

Purchases

Subscriptions (1, 3, 6 and 12 months) and the one-time lifetime purchase are sold through Apple’s App Store (StoreKit) and Google Play (Google Play Billing). Payment is processed entirely by Apple or Google. We do not receive your payment card details, billing address or store account details.

Age

Marshall VPN is not directed at children under 13. It is listed on the App Store with a 16+ age rating.

4. Quiet Escape and Capy Resort

No account, data stays on your device

Our games do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details. Game progress, settings (such as language and sound) and, in Quiet Escape, your purchase status are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us and are removed when you delete the game. The games themselves make no network requests of their own; the only network activity comes from the advertising SDK and the app stores described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Both games are free and show ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC:

  • Quiet Escape offers optional rewarded videos that you can choose to watch to receive hints, and shows full-screen ads at some moments, for example between episodes, as well as small banner or native ad placements. The optional “Remove Ads” purchase turns off the full-screen and banner ads.
  • Capy Resort shows only rewarded videos, and only when you choose to watch one for an in-game bonus.

We configure every ad request in both games for child-directed treatment and for users under the age of consent, and we limit ads to content rated G (suitable for general audiences). Under Google’s policies these settings disable personalised advertising and the use of the advertising identifier for interest-based ads.

To deliver, measure and secure ads, Google may process your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information (such as device model, operating system, language and app version), and information about the ads shown and your interactions with them. Google’s handling of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

On iOS, the game shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before ads are loaded. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the game or to Google. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

In-app purchases

Quiet Escape offers two optional purchases: “Remove Ads” (one-time) and “10 Hints”. Capy Resort has no in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details or store account details. The game receives a confirmation from the store so that it can unlock what you bought; this confirmation is kept on your device.

Permissions and analytics

The games request only internet access and network-state permissions, which are needed to load ads and complete purchases. They do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location, and they contain no analytics, crash-reporting, social-media or attribution SDKs.

5. Our website and email

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and delivered through the Cloudflare content delivery network (Cloudflare, Inc.). When you visit, these providers process your IP address and standard request information (such as browser type, the page requested and the time of the request) to deliver the pages, protect the site from abuse and keep it reliable. We do not have access to visitor-level logs.

We do not set cookies, and we use no analytics, advertising or tracking technologies on this website. Fonts are served from our own domain; no third-party font or script services are loaded. Links to the App Store, Google Play, appmarshall.com and Google’s policy pages lead to sites operated under their own privacy policies.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your message. We use this information only to reply to you, to provide support and to keep a record of our correspondence.

6. Why we use information, and on what basis

We use the information described above only to:

  • provide and maintain our apps and website, including issuing Marshall VPN access keys and unlocking purchases;
  • show advertising that keeps the free versions of our apps free;
  • answer your questions and provide support;
  • keep our services secure and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal information, we do not use it to build profiles, and we make no automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (providing the app or service you asked for and delivering your purchases); legitimate interests (keeping our apps free through advertising, securing our services and replying to you); consent where the law requires it (for example for the advertising identifier on iOS, or Google’s consent form in Marshall VPN), which you may withdraw at any time; and legal obligation where we must keep or disclose information by law.

7. Who we share information with

We share information only with the following categories of recipients, and only as needed:

  • Google — Google AdMob, to serve ads in the free versions of our apps;
  • Apple and Google — to distribute our apps and process purchases through their stores;
  • Infrastructure providers — GitHub and Cloudflare for this website, and the data-centre and hosting providers that run the Marshall VPN servers and API;
  • Professional advisers — our company secretary, accountants and auditors, where required;
  • Authorities — where required by law, or where necessary to protect our rights, our users or the public.

We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

8. International transfers

We are based in Hong Kong. Our service providers may process information in other countries, including the United States and countries in the European Economic Area. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum to them.

9. How long we keep information

  • In our apps, information stays on your device until you delete the app.
  • Marshall VPN server records (device identifier, linked devices, subscription status and purchase confirmations) are kept for as long as needed to operate the service, verify purchases and resolve disputes, and are then deleted or anonymised.
  • Email correspondence is kept for as long as needed to help you and for our business records, generally no longer than seven years.
  • Information processed by Google for advertising is retained under Google’s own policies.

10. Children

Quiet Escape and Capy Resort are suitable for all ages. They do not ask for personal information, and ads in them are configured for child-directed treatment with G-rated content, as described in section 4. Marshall VPN is not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

  • Hong Kong. Under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance you may request access to and correction of your personal data. We respond to such requests within 40 days. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (pcpd.org.hk).
  • EEA and United Kingdom. You have the rights listed above under the GDPR and UK GDPR, and the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.
  • California. You may request to know, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. An authorised agent may make a request on your behalf.

Because almost all information in our apps is stored only on your device, deleting the app deletes it. You can also:

  • change tracking permission on iOS in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID on Android in your device settings;
  • remove a device from your Marshall VPN subscription in the app’s device manager;
  • manage subscriptions on iOS in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, and on Android in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

To make a request, including deletion of Marshall VPN server records linked to your device, email support@appmarshall.com. We may ask you for information that lets us locate the relevant record and confirm that the request comes from the device in question. We respond within the time required by applicable law.

12. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the small amount of information we handle: encrypted connections (TLS) between our apps and servers, encrypted on-device storage for Marshall VPN credentials, restricted administrative access to our infrastructure, and data minimisation by design. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information entrusted to us.
