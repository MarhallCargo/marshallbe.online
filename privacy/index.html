Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information our apps and our website collect, how it is used and the choices you have. It covers all apps published by Marshall Relay Limited. We keep it as short and clear as we can.

Effective: 28 September 2026 Last updated: 28 September 2026 Applies to: Marshall VPN, Quiet Escape, Capy Resort and marshallbe.online

At a glance

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • None of our apps require an account. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number inside an app.
  • Your progress, settings and purchases are stored on your device.
  • Our free apps show ads through Google AdMob, which processes limited device data to show ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

3. Quiet Escape and Capy Resort

No account, data stays on your device

Our games do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other contact details. Your game progress, settings (such as language and sound) and purchase status are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us, and they are deleted when you delete the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Both games are free and show ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), a service of Google LLC.

  • Quiet Escape offers optional rewarded videos that you can choose to watch to get hints, and shows full-screen ads at some moments, for example between episodes. The optional “Remove Ads” purchase turns the full-screen ads off.
  • Capy Resort shows only optional rewarded videos, when you choose to watch one for a bonus.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process: your device’s advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information (such as device model, operating system, language and app version), and information about the ads shown and your interactions with them.

We configure every ad request in our games for child-directed treatment and for users under the age of consent, and we limit ads to content rated G (suitable for general audiences). Under Google’s policies, these settings turn off personalised advertising for ads in our games.

On iOS, before ads are loaded, the game shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the game or to Google. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Google’s use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Quiet Escape offers optional in-app purchases: “Remove Ads” (a one-time purchase) and a pack of hints. Capy Resort has no in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card details, billing address, Apple ID or Google account details. The game receives a confirmation from the store (such as which product was bought and its status) so it can unlock what you bought. This confirmation is checked and stored on your device.

Permissions and analytics

Our games do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location, and they do not include analytics or crash-reporting services.

4. Marshall VPN

Marshall VPN is built around privacy. In summary (full details at appmarshall.com/privacy):

  • No activity logs. We do not log your browsing activity, DNS queries, traffic content, connection timestamps or bandwidth usage on our servers.
  • No account. To create your VPN access key, the app sends our server only a unique device identifier. No name, email, phone number, Apple ID or Google account is collected.
  • Stored on your device. Your access key, server list and settings are stored on your device (in the iOS Keychain, or in encrypted storage on Android) and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Ads in the free version. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, using Google’s consent tools where required. Ads are non-personalised; the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or use the IDFA. A subscription removes ads.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We do not see your payment details.
  • Optional features. Feedback you choose to send is anonymous text only. Diagnostic logs stay on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • Third-party servers. If you import a subscription from another provider, those servers are operated by that provider, not by us.
  • Access outside the app. If you get or renew access through our subscription channel on a third-party platform, we process pseudonymous identifiers and subscription details from that platform only to issue and manage your access.

5. Our website and emails

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and delivered through Cloudflare, Inc. When you visit, these providers process your IP address and standard request information (such as browser type, the page requested and the time) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and the content of your message. We use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of our correspondence.

6. How we use information

We use information only to:

  • provide and maintain our apps and website, including unlocking purchases;
  • show ads that keep our apps free (through Google AdMob);
  • answer your questions and provide support;
  • keep our services secure and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for profiling or automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

7. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide the app or service you asked for and deliver your purchases;
  • Legitimate interests — to keep our apps free through advertising, to secure our services and to reply to you;
  • Consent — where the law requires it, for example for access to the advertising identifier on iOS or for ad consent in Marshall VPN. You can withdraw consent at any time;
  • Legal obligation — where we must keep or disclose information by law.

8. Who we share information with

We share information only with:

  • Google — Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Fonts (website);
  • Apple and Google — to distribute our apps and process purchases;
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — GitHub and Cloudflare for this website, and the providers that host our servers;
  • Professional advisers — such as our company secretary, accountants and auditors, where needed;
  • Authorities — when required by law, or where needed to protect our rights or the safety of others.

We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

9. International transfers

We are based in Hong Kong. Our service providers may process information in other countries, including the United States and countries in the European Economic Area. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses.

10. How long we keep information

  • Information stored in our apps stays on your device until you delete the app.
  • For Marshall VPN access credentials, see appmarshall.com/privacy.
  • Emails are kept as long as needed to help you and for our records, generally no longer than seven years.
  • Information processed by Google for advertising is kept under Google’s own policies.

11. Children

Quiet Escape and Capy Resort are suitable for all ages. They do not ask for personal information, and ads in them are set up for child-directed treatment with G-rated content, as described in section 3. Marshall VPN is not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority.

Most information in our apps is stored only on your device, so you can delete it by deleting the app. You can also:

  • change tracking permission on iOS in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID on Android in your device settings;
  • manage subscriptions on iOS in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, and on Android in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

To make a request, email support@appmarshall.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections and encrypted on-device storage where appropriate. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our apps, our service providers or the law change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. If the changes are significant, we will highlight them on this page.

15. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests about any of our apps or this website, email us at support@appmarshall.com.
