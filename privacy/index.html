3. Quiet Escape and Capy Resort

Data on your device

Our games need no account and ask for no personal details. Your progress, settings and purchase status are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the game. The games do not send this information to us.

Advertising

Both games are free and show ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Quiet Escape offers optional videos that give hints, and shows full-screen ads at some moments, for example between episodes, plus one ad in the main menu. The optional “Remove Ads” purchase turns off all of these except the optional videos. Capy Resort shows only optional videos that give in-game bonuses.

Every ad request in our games is marked as child-directed and for users under the age of consent, and is limited to G-rated content. Under Google’s policies, this means the ads are not personalised. To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google may process your IP address and approximate location, device and app information, and your interactions with ads, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

On iOS, the game asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency before ads load. You can change your choice in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases and permissions

Quiet Escape offers two optional purchases, “Remove Ads” and “10 Hints”. Capy Resort has no purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, and we never receive your payment details. The games only need network access for ads and purchases. They do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location, and contain no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

4. Marshall VPN

Marshall VPN has its own detailed privacy policy at appmarshall.com/privacy, which applies to that app. In brief:

  • No account is needed. The app creates a random identifier on your device, which is used to issue access and to keep track of your subscription.
  • Browsing activity, DNS queries, traffic content and connection times are not logged.
  • The free version shows non-personalised ads through Google AdMob, with Google’s consent form where required. A subscription removes the ads.
  • Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google.
  • The app is not directed at children under 13.

5. Website and email

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. These providers process your IP address and basic request data to deliver and protect the site. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on this website.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to keep a record of our correspondence.

6. Use and sharing

We use information only to provide our apps and website, deliver purchases, show ads that keep our apps free, provide support, keep our services secure and meet legal obligations. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract, our legitimate interests, your consent where the law requires it, and legal obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time.

We share information only with Google for advertising, Apple and Google for app distribution and payments, our hosting and service providers, our professional advisers, and authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell personal information or share it for others’ marketing.

7. Storage and retention

Information in our apps stays on your device until you delete the app. Emails are kept as long as needed to help you and for our records, generally no longer than seven years. Our service providers may process information outside Hong Kong, including in the United States and the European Economic Area. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

8. Children

Quiet Escape and Capy Resort are suitable for all ages. They collect no personal information, and their ads are child-directed as described in section 3. Marshall VPN is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to a data protection authority. In Hong Kong this is the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data.

To make a request, email support@appmarshall.com. We respond to data access requests within 40 days. Because our apps store data on your device, deleting an app also deletes its data.

10. Security and changes

We protect information with encrypted connections, secure on-device storage and limited access to our systems. We may update this policy when our apps or the law change. The date at the top shows the current version, and significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
